CRAFT & DISCIPLINE

Skills, Methodologies & How I Build

From early exploratory research to precise interaction craft and empirical testing, here is how I untangle complexity into clear, delightful systems.

01

Discover & Research

Understanding the real human behind every click. I dig deep into motivations, behavioral frictions, and contextual constraints through structured qualitative and quantitative inquiry.

User Interviews Contextual Inquiry Field & Diary Studies Competitor Benchmarking Empathy Mapping User Personas & Archetypes Deductive & Inductive Coding Literature Review
02

Structure & Architecture

Giving clarity to chaos. Before moving pixels, I map out objects, relationships, navigation mental models, and robust end-to-end user journeys.

Object-Oriented UX (OOUX) Information Architecture (IA) Task & User Flows Wireframing (Lo-Fi to Hi-Fi) Mental Model Mapping Content Strategy Semantics & Semiotics System Logic Diagrams
03

Craft & Interaction Design

Transforming wireframes into tactile, responsive, and aesthetically stunning interfaces with deliberate micro-interactions, layout rhythm, and tokenized design systems.

Interactive Prototyping Design Systems & Tokens Micro-Interactions Motion & Easing Choreography Automotive Cockpit Design Multimodal Interfaces Responsive Web & Native Apps Visual Hierarchy & Typography
04

Test, Validate & Measure

Validating assumptions with empirical user feedback. I run iterative usability evaluations, cognitive walkthroughs, and quantitative benchmarks to prove real-world usability.

Usability Testing (Moderated/Unmoderated) A/B & Multivariate Testing Cognitive Walkthroughs Heuristic Evaluations (Nielsen) System Usability Scale (SUS) Accessibility Audits (WCAG 2.1 AA) Task Completion Analysis Iterative Synthesis Loops
