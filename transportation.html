Bus Transportation Management

MIT ADT manages 37 university and third-party buses, yet daily operations depended on WhatsApp, Excel, and calls. I designed a unified transport management system with an administrative dashboard and student mobile experience, centralising routes, schedules, fees, requests, live tracking, and delay notifications while improving coordination between the university, students, and Tarawade Transport.

Project type UI/UX Case Study - System Design
Domain Campus Mobility / Transportation
Duration 3 Weeks
Platform Desktop Dashboard + Mobile App
Users Transport Department, Students, Faculty and Coordinators
Methods Stakeholder Mapping, Swimlane Model, User Stories, Object-Action Mapping, Competitor Analysis, Information Architecture
Outcome Unified transport management experience
