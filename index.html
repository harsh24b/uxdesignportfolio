A Little More About Me

Pune, India • Designing at the intersection of HCI & AI

Hi, I am Harsh, User Experience Designer with a strong interest in building thoughtful, scalable digital products that balance usability, clarity, and innovation. My work combines research-driven problem solving, structured design systems, and visual communication to create seamless user experiences.

I’ve led initiatives as Vice Chair of the ACM SIGCHI Student Chapter and contributed to HCIPAI, strengthening my leadership and collaborative skills.
