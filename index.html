Where Craft Meets Validation

Experience & honors

Helped establish the UX research foundation at Škoda Auto Volkswagen India by evaluating research infrastructure and agencies. Conducted automotive user studies and competitor benchmarking to deliver quantitative insights and strategic recommendations for digital mobility systems.

Redesigned the Makist website by restructuring visual hierarchy and applying cognitive-ergonomics principles to layout and button placement, cutting key user flows and steps across core pages. Built brand guidelines and a design system of reusable components, and delivered end-to-end high fidelity desktop and mobile UI screens.

Won first place in two consecutive editions of the Samsung PRISM Hackathons through rapid user discovery and prototyping. In 2026, designed Guardian Intelligence, an Agentic AI system making children’s screen time safer, developmentally positive, and transparent for parents.

As Vice Chair of MITID ACM SIGCHI, the premier international association for human-computer interaction, I steer operational strategy and lead student teams to organize campus design initiatives, bridging our academic community with global research.

Contributed to the HCIPAI Education Initiative for India HCI 2026 by leading visual communication and digital outreach. Designed key graphic assets and managed campaign strategy to connect academic HCI discourse with emerging designers nationwide.
